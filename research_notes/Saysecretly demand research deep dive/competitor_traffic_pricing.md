# Competitor Reality Check: Traffic and Pricing by Candidate Space (for saysecretly.com)

Method notes (apply to every number below):
- All traffic figures are from Similarweb's free public pages (`similarweb.com/website/<domain>/`), fetched 2026-10-27. Every page was labeled **September 2026**. They are third-party estimates. For small sites (<50K visits) they are often off by 2x or more.
- Similarweb's free page puts the headline visit number under a heading "Total Visits Last 3 Months" and doesn't say whether it is one month or three. Similarweb's convention is that the headline figure is the latest month, so it is treated here as **approximate monthly visits (Sep 2026)**. The writer should present it as "~X visits/month (Similarweb est., Sep 2026)".
- The free pages show only the share of the #1 traffic channel. Channels 2 and 3 appear as a rank without a percentage. A full 3-6 month monthly series is not shown, so "trend" means the stated month-over-month change plus, where shown, the 3-month change in global rank.
- "Est. US visits" = visits x US share. I calculated these, and they are rough.
- **Blocked or unavailable estimators:** hypestat.com returned HTTP 403 to curl. Semrush and Ahrefs free pages were not tried because Similarweb worked. Similarweb had no data for getsendit.com visits (no country data either) or incogneato.com (whose domain no longer resolves in DNS: SERVFAIL). Similarweb's safehotline.com chart did not load.
- **Seasonality warning:** September is the off-season for Secret Santa and gift exchange (Elfster and DrawNames peak Nov-Dec) and for many holiday group cards. Their September numbers understate annual demand.

## 1. Traffic by space: monthly visits, trend, US share, traffic sources

### Takeaway
Among all the candidates, the largest US web traffic is in **group cards/invitations** (Punchbowl ~4.1M US visits/mo; Kudoboard plus GroupGreeting ~0.8M US) and **gift exchange/wishlists** (Elfster ~1.4M, Giftster ~1.0M, DrawNames ~0.5M US, even in September). Anonymous Q&A is large but mostly non-US and declining (NGL 10.3M, only ~12% US, -25% MoM). The web footprint of anonymous workplace feedback, campus anonymous apps, and Tea is small (tens of thousands of visits). In those spaces demand runs through apps, sales and B2B channels rather than web search.

### Inferences
- **Anonymous Q&A on the web is a link-in-bio social mechanic, not a search market.** NGL's #1 channel is organic social, and its users are mostly outside the US. It shrank 25% MoM and its global rank has slipped over 3 months. Sendit, Whisper and Sarahah have almost no web presence. A newcomer here would need a viral loop (Instagram/Snap stories), not SEO.
- **Group cards and gift exchange have high US share (60-90%) and Direct/Mail-heavy traffic.** That points to repeat and invite-driven usage: each organizer pulls in many participants. This is the classic viral-B2C loop a "say it secretly" brand could ride (anonymous notes in group cards, anonymous Secret Santa messaging).
- Campus anonymous apps (Fizz, Sidechat) and Tea are app-first. Their web traffic (<100K) understates their user bases, so web visits are a poor proxy there.
- The workplace-feedback vendors' websites get small traffic (<70K/mo even for NAVEX). That is typical of sales-led B2B. Demand is real but bought through procurement and compliance requirements, not discovered by consumers.

### Gaps
- No monthly series for 3-6 months: Similarweb's free page hides the per-month chart values. Trend is limited to MoM change and 3-month rank change.
- Channel percentages beyond the #1 channel are paywalled.
- No reliable web traffic for getsendit.com, safehotline.com, whiteelephantonline.com or sendit.app. Evite group cards, sendusacard, Memento, matter, polly.ai and other vent apps were not measured, to stay within the tool-call budget.
- No 2026 user count for Tea or for AWDTSG groups.

## 3. Opportunity map: big traffic with weak or old incumbents vs. strong incumbents

### Takeaway
The best openings are **holiday gift exchange** and **SMB anonymous feedback**. Gift exchange has big, seasonal, US-heavy traffic, but the incumbents are old, free, ad- or affiliate-funded sites (Elfster 2006-era, DrawNames, Giftster), and there is no anonymous-messaging angle. SMB anonymous feedback has small, sales-led incumbents and one defunct player. Spaces to avoid head-on: **group cards** (Punchbowl, Kudoboard and GroupGreeting are entrenched, Direct/Mail-loyal and monetized), and **anonymous Q&A and dating-gossip apps** (declining, mostly non-US, plus a legal and safety minefield, as the FTC/NGL and Tea breaches show).

### Cited Findings
- Elfster (~1.4M US visits even in September, rising +20% MoM into the season) runs on affiliate revenue only, not paid features — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/elfster.com/); [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/platforms/how-elfster-is-capitalizing-on-digital-gifting-to-entice-retail-brand-partners/)
- DrawNames' traffic jumped +242.6% MoM in September, which shows the steep seasonal ramp — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/drawnames.com/)
- The exact-match domain secretsanta.com gets only ~3.5K visits, so a keyword domain alone doesn't capture this demand — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/secretsanta.com/)
- Group-card incumbents' visits are 71-74% Direct (Kudoboard, GroupGreeting), which signals a loyal, invite-driven base — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/kudoboard.com/), [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/groupgreeting.com/)
- NGL: -25% MoM, ~12% US — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/ngl.link/); FTC $5M settlement — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NGL_(app))
- Tea: removed from the App Store, breaches, multiple class actions — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea_(app)); [unanswered.io](https://unanswered.io/guide/has-the-tea-app-been-sued)
- FutureMe has ~623K visits but only ~29% US, 0% paid search, and a 20+-year-old product — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/futureme.org/)

### Inferences
- **Opportunity (moderate):** "Secret Santa plus anonymous notes" for Q4 2026. The demand is proven (millions of US visits Nov-Dec), the incumbents are dated and free, and the product fits the "secretly" brand naturally. The catch is that it's free-to-play, so monetization would come from affiliate links or a premium corporate tier like White Elephant Online's $69+/event.
- **Opportunity (niche):** time-capsule letters. There is one dominant old player and moderate global traffic, but the US slice is small (~178K/mo).
- **Adjacent and defensible:** anonymous messages inside group cards and tributes, as a feature wedge rather than a head-on clone of Kudoboard.
- **Avoid as a core business:** anonymous Q&A for teens (declining, regulatory scrutiny), dating-safety callouts (defamation, privacy and breach liability), and enterprise hotlines (NAVEX-dominated, compliance-heavy, sales-led).

### Gaps
- No search-volume data (keyword volumes for "secret santa generator" and similar) was gathered here. It would help confirm the size of the seasonal demand.

## 4. Revenue signals

### Takeaway
Hard revenue numbers are scarce. The best signals are NGL's 2022 app spend (~$2.4M, Sensor Tower), Elfster's affiliate model with a claimed 17M users a year, and Kudoboard's PeakSpan growth investment. Treat everything else as unknown.

### Cited Findings
- NGL: ~$2.4M in user spending by Jul 2022 (Sensor Tower via TechCrunch), against Appfigures' estimate of $375K net revenue by Jun 2022. These are conflicting third-party estimates — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/07/11/anonymous-social-ngl-tops-15m-installs-2-4m-in-revenue-as-users-complain-about-being-scammed); [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20220624/1-ngl-is-still-the-hottest-app-in-the-us-but-is-it-making-money). Low-medium confidence, and dated.
- Kudoboard: founded 2015; seed from The Idea Village; PeakSpan Capital investment in 2021 (amount undisclosed). This comes from a wiki-style source, so confidence is low — [Wikitia](https://wikitia.com/wiki/Kudoboard). SpendHound puts its SMB spend at ~$126/yr per customer — [SpendHound](https://www.spendhound.com/marketplace/kudoboard-pricing)
- Elfster: claims 17M users a year; affiliate revenue; added >25 retail partners for the 2021 season — [Multichannel Merchant](https://multichannelmerchant.com/press-releases/elfster-adds-more-than-25-retail-partners-ahead-of-2021-holiday-season). No revenue figure is published.
- Tea: ~4M users and 6.1M iOS downloads by Oct 2025 (Appfigures). No revenue figure was found — [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1926512/what-is-the-tea-app-explained/)

### Inferences
- If Similarweb's estimates are right, Kudoboard's ~720K visits/mo at $7-$20 per board, plus B2B subscriptions, suggest a multi-million-dollar ARR business. This is speculative and not sourced.

### Gaps
- Latka, Crunchbase and PitchBook were not queried. There is no revenue or headcount data for GroupGreeting, Punchbowl, Tribute, Giftster, DrawNames, FutureMe, AllVoices or FaceUp. Job-posting signals were not gathered.
