# Anonymous Social Apps: History (Successes/Failures) and US Legal/Regulatory Landscape

Research date: 2026-10-27. US focus. About 21 search/fetch calls. All facts carry inline sources; anything I could not source is under "Gaps".

## Q1. App-by-app history: peaks, business models, growth drivers, causes of decline

### Takeaway
Most consumer anonymous apps followed the same pattern. A viral loop (a campus or school network, or Instagram/Snapchat link-sharing) produced explosive growth in weeks. Then bullying or threats, store and platform removals, regulator action, or simple novelty fade cut them down within roughly 6–24 months. Monetization was mostly "pay to see who sent it" weekly subscriptions (NGL, Sendit), and that model later drew FTC deception charges. Acquirers bought these apps for teams or teen reach and shut them down within months (tbh, Gas).

### Cited Findings

**Secret (Jan 2014 – Apr 2015)**
- Co-founder/CEO David Byttow shut the app down in April 2015, about 16 months after launch. He said it no longer matched his vision and returned the remaining capital to investors instead of pivoting, which was unusual. — [CNN Money, 2015-04-29](https://money.cnn.com/2015/04/29/technology/secret-closes-investors/); [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2015/the-secrets-out-annoymous-social-app-is-shutting-down-and-returning-capital-to-investors/)
- Secret raised about $35M from Index, Redpoint, SV Angel, Garry Tan, Alexis Ohanian and others. It reported 15M+ users. Critics had raised concerns about abusive content, and a late redesign was compared to Yik Yak. — [Computerworld](https://www.computerworld.com/article/1367920/secret-an-app-for-anonymous-posts-shuts-down.html); [CNN Money](https://money.cnn.com/2015/04/29/technology/secret-closes-investors/)

**Whisper (2012 – )**
- In March 2020 the Washington Post reported, from researchers at Twelve Security, that Whisper (owned by MediaLab) had left about 900M records exposed in a non-password-protected Elasticsearch database covering 2012 onward. Records held age, gender, ethnicity, hometown, nickname, group memberships and approximate last-post locations that could point to schools or workplaces. MediaLab downplayed it, saying the posts were meant to be public. — [Engadget, 2020-03-11](https://www.engadget.com/2020-03-11-whisper-open-database-exposed-users.html); [Sophos](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/blog/confessions-app-whisper-spills-almost-a-billion-records)
- Lesson: "anonymous" posts with rich metadata can still be re-identified. A data leak hits an anonymity brand especially hard.

**Yik Yak (original 2013–2017; relaunch Aug 2021; Sidechat merger Mar 2023)**
- Growth came from a geofenced, hyperlocal campus feed. The company raised about $73M and was valued around $400M at its 2014 peak. — [TechCrunch, 2021-08-16](https://techcrunch.com/2021/08/16/yik-yak-is-back/)
- Valuation estimates vary ($300M per a Cornell course blog). The Cornell blog also says the app's App Store rank fell from No. 3 overall (2014) to No. 447 (2016). — [Cornell INFO 2040 blog](https://blogs.cornell.edu/info2040/2016/11/10/yik-yak-and-the-network-effect/)
- Yik Yak was linked to cyberbullying and threats of violence. Schools sought bans, so the company geofenced most US middle and high schools to block use. One secondary source says MAU fell to about 264K by 2016, roughly 75% year over year (lower-quality source). — [Failory](https://www.failory.com/cemetery/yik-yak); [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/what-happened-to-yik-yak/)
- It shut down in April 2017. Square paid about $1M for some engineers and IP. — [Wikipedia: Yik Yak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yik_Yak); [Failory](https://www.failory.com/cemetery/yik-yak)
- Relaunch, Aug 2021: new, initially unnamed owners; onboarding warns of a "one strike and you're out" policy for bullying and threats. — [TechCrunch, 2021-08-16](https://techcrunch.com/2021/08/16/yik-yak-is-back/)
- March 2023: Sidechat effectively acquired Yik Yak, which TechCrunch described as acqui-hire-like. Users were first pushed to Sidechat, then Yik Yak was reskinned onto the same backend. Users protested because Sidechat required a school email, unlike the open, fully anonymous Yik Yak. — [TechCrunch, 2023-03-16](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/16/anonymous-app-sidechat-picks-up-rival-yik-yak-and-users-arent-happy/); [TechCrunch, 2023-04-03](https://techcrunch.com/2023/04/03/yik-yak-users-are-protesting-the-apps-makeover-after-acquisition-by-sidechat/amp/)

**Sidechat / Fizz (campus-verified anonymous, 2020s)**
- 2026 roundups describe Sidechat as a campus-first anonymous app with threads organized by college. Fizz runs private school communities with anonymous posting plus a student marketplace and claims "hundreds of thousands" of users (vendor and listicle sources, unverified). — [Backlinko 2026](https://backlinko.com/new-social-media-platforms); [Tide](https://tidecampus.com/blog/best-apps-to-make-friends-in-college)
- New entrants keep appearing, for example "YourSecret" (press-release launch, March 31, 2026) as an anonymous chat app for US college students. — [FinancialContent/ACCESS Newswire](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/accwirecq-2026-3-31-yoursecret-launches-the-new-anonymous-chat-app-for-us-college-students)

**Sarahah (2016/2017 – removed Feb 2018)**
- On Jan 12, 2018, Australian parent Katrina Collins started a Change.org petition after her 13-year-old daughter got abusive messages. It drew about 470K signatures. Apple and then Google removed the app around February 2018 (one blog says March), and existing users could keep using it. — [Change.org petition update](https://www.change.org/p/12898804/u/22412492); [Wikipedia: Sarahah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarahah); [TechPP, 2018-02-27](https://techpp.com/2018/02/27/sarahah-exit-play-store-app-store/)
- A widely repeated "300M users" figure is unsourced, so treat it as unverified. — [Gizbot](https://www.gizbot.com/amphtml/apps/news/sarahah-app-removed-from-apple-and-google-stores-after-allegations-of-cyber-bullying-048648.html)

**tbh (2017 – Jul 2018; acquired by Facebook)**
- tbh was an anonymous positive-polling app for high schoolers, rolled out school by school. Facebook acquired it in October 2017 and shut it down on July 2, 2018 (along with Hello and Moves), citing "low usage". — [TechCrunch, 2018-07-02](https://techcrunch.com/2018/07/02/facebook-is-shutting-down-hello-moves-and-the-anonymous-teen-app-tbh-due-to-low-usage); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2018/07/02/facebook-shuts-down-tbh-hello-moves/)
- Reported traction: 5M+ installs and 2.5M DAU before the acquisition (Mobile Dev Memo). Sensor Tower counted 6.4M total installs. — [Mobile Dev Memo](https://mobiledevmemo.com/the-importance-of-facebook-shuttering-tbh-9-months-after-acquiring-it/)
- The deal price was undisclosed. One newsletter claims about $100M (single source, unverified).

**Gas (Aug 2022 – Nov 2023; acquired by Discord)**
- Gas was Nikita Bier's tbh remake (anonymous compliments polls). It reached 1M DAU within about 2 months of its August 2022 launch. Downloads peaked in November 2022 and then fell sharply, for about 7M all-time downloads (data.ai). — [TechCrunch, 2023-10-19](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/19/discord-kills-gas-anonymous-compliments-app-bought-nine-months-ago)
- Discord acquired Gas in January 2023 for an undisclosed sum. In October 2023 it announced a shutdown for November 7, 2023. Discord's CEO told staff the deal was mainly for engineering talent and that Gas would run only while growth lasted. — [TechCrunch, 2023-10-19](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/19/discord-kills-gas-anonymous-compliments-app-bought-nine-months-ago)

**YOLO and LMK (Snapchat-integrated anonymous Q&A)**
- In May 2021 the estate of Carson Bride (16, Oregon, died by suicide in June 2020 after anonymous bullying via YOLO/LMK), his mother, and the Tyler Clementi Foundation sued Snap, YOLO and LMK in N.D. Cal. Snap then suspended YOLO's and LMK's Snap Kit integrations. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/snapchat-yolo-lmk-suspended-lawsuit-155401305.html); [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/class-action/snap-puts-yolo-lmk-on-hold-after-teen-bullying-class-suit-filed)
- Growth then shifted to rivals. Sendit gained about 3.5M downloads after Snap suspended YOLO and LMK. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2184502)

**Sendit (Iconic Hearts)**
- Business model: a "Diamond Membership" at up to $9.99/week, marketed as revealing anonymous senders.
- Sept 2025: DOJ, on FTC referral, sued Iconic Hearts and CEO Hunter Rice. The complaint alleges:
  - COPPA violations, including 116,000 users self-declared under 13 in 2022, with data collection continuing even after parents complained
  - fake provocative messages presented as coming from users' contacts
  - that Diamond did not actually reveal senders
  - undisclosed recurring weekly billing
  — [FTC press release, Sept 2025](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-alleges-sendit-app-its-ceo-unlawfully-collected-personal-data-children-deceived-users-about); [Malwarebytes, Oct 2025](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2025/10/sendit-app-tricked-kids-harvested-their-data-and-faked-messages-ftc-claims)

**NGL (launched Dec 2021)**
- By July 2022 NGL had 15M+ global installs and about $2.4M in revenue (Sensor Tower). It made money through a $9.99/week subscription for "hints" about senders. App reviews complained of bots, fake messages and scams. Growth was heavily Android-driven in India and Indonesia. — [TechCrunch, 2022-07-11](https://techcrunch.com/2022/07/11/anonymous-social-ngl-tops-15m-installs-2-4m-in-revenue-as-users-complain-about-being-scammed)
- Current Sensor Tower snapshot pages show sizable ongoing downloads and revenue (for example, about 700K downloads and $600K revenue in a recent month on US iOS). These are undated estimates. — [Sensor Tower NGL US iOS](https://app.sensortower.com/overview/1596550932?country=US)
- See Q2 for the 2024 FTC/LA DA action.

**Wizz (teen friend-finding/chat app)**
- Apple and Google removed Wizz around January 30, 2024, after the National Center on Sexual Exploitation flagged sextortion. NCRI said some victims were targeted "within minutes of joining", and there were complaints about porn ads shown to minors. Wizz was later reinstated (date not found). — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/social-media/wizz-tinder-app-aimed-teens-removed-apple-google-stores-rcna136607); [Infosecurity Magazine](https://infosecurity-magazine.com/news/wizz-removed-apple-google-stores); [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/news/exclusive/abuse-teenage-dating-app/)

### Inferences
- **Growth drivers.** Three recurring mechanisms account for nearly every breakout:
  - A closed local graph: Yik Yak's geofence, tbh and Gas going school by school, Sidechat/Fizz requiring a .edu email.
  - Distribution through Instagram Stories or Snapchat: NGL, Sendit, YOLO, LMK, Sarahah.
  - A curiosity hook ("who sent this?") that also became the paywall.
- **Decline drivers.** The same mechanisms decay fast:
  - Novelty fade: Gas downloads peaked about 3 months after launch; tbh shut down within 9 months of acquisition.
  - Abuse among minors brings school bans, parent petitions and store removals (Yik Yak, Sarahah, Wizz).
  - Engineered engagement (fake messages) plus weekly "reveal" subscriptions bring FTC deception and COPPA actions (NGL, Sendit).
- **Acquirers.** They treated these apps as talent or teen-reach plays, not durable businesses. Both Facebook (tbh) and Discord (Gas) shut theirs down within about a year.

### Gaps
- No reliable peak MAU/DAU or revenue found for Whisper, Tellonym, Sidechat, Fizz or Yik Yak 2.0.
- No reliable data on Tellonym's US history. It was not covered in my searches.
- "Tolan" did not come up as an anonymous app. My understanding, unverified in this session, is that it is an AI-companion app, so it may be the wrong comparator.
- No sourced numbers for the 2021 Yik Yak relaunch.
- Gas and tbh acquisition prices are undisclosed.

## Q2. The 2024 FTC + LA DA action against NGL

### Takeaway
In July 2024 the FTC and the Los Angeles County DA settled with NGL Labs and its co-founders. Terms were $5M plus the first-ever US order barring a digital platform from serving minors (under 18). The core allegations were fake "from your friends" messages, false claims about AI anti-bullying moderation, and deceptive weekly subscriptions. The FTC brought the same playbook against Sendit in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Settlement (announced July 9, 2024):
  - $5M payment
  - NGL barred from offering the app to users under 18, the first time the US ordered a platform to stop serving minors
  - age gate required
  - deletion of data on under-13s collected without parental consent
  - no misrepresenting message senders or AI capabilities
  - clear disclosure of recurring charges, express consent before billing, and easy cancellation

  — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/ftc-bans-anonymous-messaging-app-ngl-for-minors-rcna160897); [law.co.il summary, 2024-07-11](https://www.law.co.il/en/news/2024/07/11/ftc-settles-charges-against-messaging-app-unfair-marketing/)
- Allegations:
  - NGL sent automated or fake messages that looked like they came from users' social media friends
  - it falsely claimed AI moderation filtered cyberbullying
  - it used deceptive tactics to sell NGL Pro, which promised "hints" about the sender

  — [Scripps News](https://scrippsnews.com/science-and-tech/social-media/anonymous-messaging-app-allegedly-sent-fake-messages-exposed-kids-to-cyberbullying); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/ftc-bans-anonymous-messaging-app-ngl-for-minors-rcna160897)
- Co-founder Joao Figueiredo said many of the allegations about the youth of the user base were "factually incorrect". — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/ftc-bans-anonymous-messaging-app-ngl-for-minors-rcna160897)
- Follow-on case: in September 2025 the FTC/DOJ sued Sendit over nearly identical conduct (fake messages, an unmasking subscription that didn't unmask, recurring billing, under-13 data). — [FTC, Sept 2025](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-alleges-sendit-app-its-ceo-unlawfully-collected-personal-data-children-deceived-users-about)

### Inferences
- Lessons for a new product:
  - Never generate synthetic messages that look like real users.
  - Never sell an "unmask" feature that doesn't truly unmask. Selling real unmasking would also defeat the anonymity promise and create safety risk.
  - Any safety or AI-moderation claims must be accurate and provable.
  - Subscriptions need clear recurring-charge disclosure, express consent and one-click cancellation.
  - An 18+ policy with a real age gate is now the regulator-endorsed default for anonymous messaging.
- Founders were named personally in both NGL and Sendit, so individual liability is a real risk.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch the FTC complaint or order PDF for NGL. Exact age-gate mechanics (for example, whether a neutral age screen is enough) were not verified.
- The status and outcome of the Sendit case as of October 2026 were not found.

## Q3. Snap's 2022 anonymous-app ban and Apple App Store rules (what's allowed now)

### Takeaway
Since March 17, 2022, Snap Kit bans third-party apps that let users message anonymously, and friend-finding apps on Snap Kit must be 18+. Apple's Guideline 1.2 says UGC apps "used primarily for... random or anonymous chat... or bullying... do not belong on the App Store", and 1.1.6 rejects anonymous or prank SMS and calling apps. Anonymous features are still possible, but they need filtering, reporting, blocking and published contact info, and the app cannot be primarily random or anonymous chat.

### Cited Findings
- Snap's March 17, 2022 Snap Kit policy:
  - bans third-party apps that let users communicate without visible, registered identities, because the abuse risks were judged impossible to mitigate acceptably
  - requires friend-finding apps to be age-gated to 18+
  - affected about 2% (anonymous ban) and 3% (friend-finding) of more than 1,500 Snap Kit developers
  - gave existing developers 30 days to comply; immediate for new developers
  - adds six-monthly reviews

  — [Snap Values, "Announcing New Policies for Snap's Developer Platform"](https://values.snap.com/news/announcing-new-policies-for-snaps-developer-platform); [TechCrunch, 2022-03-17](https://techcrunch.com/2022/03/17/following-suicides-and-lawsuits-snapchat-restricts-apps-building-on-its-platform-with-new-policies)
- In May 2022 enforcement was still incomplete, with some banned or restricted apps still non-compliant. — [TechCrunch, 2022-05-17](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/17/snapchats-stricter-policies-for-anonymous-apps-and-friend-finders-arent-yet-fully-enforced)
- Apple Guideline 1.2 (current text):
  - names "anonymous bullying" as a UGC risk
  - requires a filter for objectionable material, a reporting mechanism with timely responses, user blocking, and published contact info
  - says apps "used primarily for pornographic content, Chatroulette-style experiences, random or anonymous chat, objectification of real people (such as 'hot-or-not' voting), making physical threats, or bullying do not belong on the App Store and may be removed without notice"
  - lets Apple remove apps that fail to remediate after notice

  — [Apple App Store Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- Apple 1.1.6: "Apps that enable anonymous or prank phone calls or SMS/MMS messaging will be rejected." Guideline 5.1.4 requires COPPA compliance and a privacy policy for apps that collect data from minors, including through chat. — [Apple App Store Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- In practice, Apple and Google have removed anonymous or teen apps after advocacy pressure (Sarahah 2018, Wizz 2024). — [TechPP](https://techpp.com/2018/02/27/sarahah-exit-play-store-app-store/); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/social-media/wizz-tinder-app-aimed-teens-removed-apple-google-stores-rcna136607)

### Inferences
- NGL, Sidechat and others remain listed, so "anonymous Q&A or posting with moderation" is tolerated. "Random or anonymous chat" as the primary function is not.
- Building on the web first, or tying anonymity to a specific purpose (feedback, confession to a known group, workplace reporting), lowers App Review risk.
- Instagram/Snap link-sharing growth loops are fragile. Snap Kit integration for anonymous messaging is closed, and Instagram link stickers depend on Meta policy.

### Gaps
- Google Play's specific UGC and anonymous-app policy text was not fetched.
- Meta's policy on anonymous apps using Instagram link stickers was not researched.
- No revision date was visible on the Apple guidelines page.

## Q4. US law: COPPA (2025 update), Section 230 limits, state minors' laws, FTC Act, whistleblower and anonymous-reporting laws

### Takeaway
- **COPPA:** the amended rule took effect June 23, 2025, with full compliance due April 22, 2026, so enforcement exposure is live now.
- **Section 230:** it still shields hosting of third-party content. However, the Ninth Circuit lets claims proceed for an app's own product design (Lemmon v. Snap, 2021) and for its promises about moderation or unmasking (Bride v. YOLO, 2024).
- **State laws:** minors' social-media laws are a patchwork, mostly enjoined. The notable exception is Florida HB 3, which the Eleventh Circuit allowed (October 2026). Mississippi's law is also enforceable while litigation continues.
- **B2B:** anonymous reporting is legally required or rewarded (SOX §301; Federal Sentencing Guidelines), which creates a durable market.

### Cited Findings

**COPPA**
- The FTC's final COPPA Rule amendments were published April 22, 2025, took effect June 23, 2025, and set a general compliance deadline of April 22, 2026. Safe-harbor programs had earlier deadlines (about October 22, 2025). — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/privacy-protection/1626104/childrens-online-privacy-protection-act-amendments-effective-june-23-2025); [Finnegan](https://www.finnegan.com/en/insights/articles/coppas-amended-rule-is-now-in-full-effect-what-operators-need-to-know.html); [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/unpacking-ftcs-coppa-amendments-what-you-need-know)
- Sendit (2025) shows COPPA actual-knowledge risk: users who self-declare as under 13 must not be kept or tracked without parental consent. — [FTC, Sept 2025](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-alleges-sendit-app-its-ceo-unlawfully-collected-personal-data-children-deceived-users-about)

**Section 230 limits**
- *Lemmon v. Snap* (9th Cir., May 4, 2021, No. 20-55295): Section 230 does not bar a negligent-design claim aimed at the provider's own product architecture (the Speed Filter plus rewards), because that treats Snap as a manufacturer, not a publisher. — [Covington Inside Privacy](https://www.insideprivacy.com/data/ninth-circuit-denies-section-230-defense-in-products-liability-case/); [Eric Goldman blog](https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2021/05/the-ninth-circuits-confusing-ruling-over-snapchats-speed-filter-lemmon-v-snap.htm)
- *Estate of Bride v. YOLO Technologies* (9th Cir., August 2024): a mixed ruling.
  - Section 230 did not bar misrepresentation claims based on YOLO's in-app promises that users would be "banned for any inappropriate usage" and that it would unmask anyone who sent harassing messages.
  - It did bar the claims that the anonymous-messaging design was defective.

  — [Eric Goldman blog, 2024-08](https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2024/08/when-it-comes-to-section-230-the-ninth-circuit-is-a-chaos-agent-estate-of-bride-v-yolo.htm); [EPIC](https://epic.org/ninth-circuit-recognizes-section-230-not-a-get-out-of-jail-free-card-for-tech-companies-that-allegedly-lie-to-users/)
- Later in Bride:
  - March 2025: a district ruling held the First Amendment doesn't protect descriptions of moderation practices.
  - On remand YOLO defaulted, and a March 2026 ruling (C.D. Cal.) rejected class certification.
  - Commentators note tension with *Doe v. Grindr*, where a generic "safe and secure" statement was treated as a description of moderation policy.

  — [Goldman, 2025-03](https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2025/03/first-amendment-doesnt-apply-to-descriptions-of-content-moderation-practices-bride-v-snap.htm); [Goldman, 2026-05](https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2026/05/the-yolo-remand-shows-why-the-9th-circuit-should-stop-carving-up-section-230-bride-v-snap.htm)

**State minors' social-media laws (status as found)**
- **Florida HB 3** bars accounts for under-14s and requires parental consent for 14–15-year-olds on covered platforms. On October 14, 2026 the Eleventh Circuit vacated the preliminary injunction, finding the law likely constitutional as a content-neutral restriction. The court had earlier stayed the injunction, allowing enforcement. — [Florida Phoenix, 2026-10-14](https://floridaphoenix.com/2026/10/14/appeals-court-lifts-block-on-florida-law-restricting-social-media-access-for-teens/); [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/11th-circuit-sides-with-florida-over-social-media-ban-for-minors/); [11th Cir. opinion](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/202511881.pdf); [Volokh/Reason](https://reason.com/volokh/2026/10/14/florida-addictive-features-social-media-restrictions-on-minors-likely-constitutional-eleventh-circuit-holds/)
- **Mississippi:** the Supreme Court declined to block enforcement while litigation proceeds. Justice Kavanaugh said the law was likely unconstitutional but that NetChoice hadn't met the bar for emergency relief. A later district order paused it against certain NetChoice members. — [ABC7](https://abc7.com/post/supreme-court-allows-enforcement-mississippi-social-media-age-verification-law/17536668/)
- **Arkansas** Act 900 (its second attempt) was blocked on April 20, 2026. — [IDTechWire](https://idtechwire.com/federal-judge-blocks-arkansas-social-media-age-verification-law/); [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2467688)
- **Georgia's** under-16 parental-consent law was preliminarily enjoined. Coverage described it as one of about eight states with such laws blocked (Ohio among them). — [WABE](https://www.wabe.org/judge-blocks-georgias-social-media-age-verification-law-citing-free-speech-concerns/)
- **Tennessee's** law has been in effect since January 2025. The Sixth Circuit revived NetChoice's challenge. — [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/articles/sixth-circuit-revives-tech-groups-challenge-to-tennessee-social-media-law)
- **Texas:** a search summary referenced a December 2025 First Amendment injunction against a Texas law, likely the App Store Accountability Act (SB 2420). Not verified against a primary source. — [IDTechWire](https://idtechwire.com/federal-judge-blocks-arkansas-social-media-age-verification-law/)

**FTC Act §5 (deception and unfairness)**
- NGL (2024) and Sendit (2025) show the FTC's theories against anonymous apps:
  - fake messages are deceptive
  - false safety or AI-moderation claims are deceptive
  - hidden recurring charges and hard cancellation are unlawful (the NGL order requires express consent and simple cancellation)
  - marketing to minors is unfair

  — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/ftc-bans-anonymous-messaging-app-ngl-for-minors-rcna160897); [FTC Sendit release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-alleges-sendit-app-its-ceo-unlawfully-collected-personal-data-children-deceived-users-about)

**Anonymous reporting / whistleblower laws (B2B tailwind)**
- **SOX §301** (with SEC rules for exchange-listed companies) requires audit committees to set up procedures for employees to submit concerns about questionable accounting or auditing matters "confidential[ly], anonymous[ly]". — [FindLaw](https://corporate.findlaw.com/litigation-disputes/sarbanes-oxley-hotline-procedures-who-should-be-doing-the.html)
- **US Sentencing Guidelines §8B2.1(b)(5)(C):** an effective compliance program includes a reporting system with "mechanisms that allow for anonymous or confidential reporting". Meeting it lowers culpability scores and penalties. — [USSC public comment](https://www.ussc.gov/sites/default/files/pdf/amendment-process/public-hearings-and-meetings/20040317-19/ERCFellowscomment.pdf); [legalclarity.org](https://legalclarity.org/what-is-an-ethics-hotline-reporting-anonymity-rights/)
- No general federal mandate requires private (non-listed) companies to run hotlines. They adopt them to meet Guidelines or DOJ compliance expectations. — [legalclarity.org](https://legalclarity.org/what-is-an-ethics-hotline-reporting-anonymity-rights/)

### Inferences
- **Section 230 design.** Section 230 will likely still cover the anonymous-messaging feature itself (Bride). Every safety promise in onboarding or marketing, though, is a potential non-230 misrepresentation claim, so promise only what is enforced. Novel engagement features that push users toward harm risk Lemmon-style design claims.
- **Minors.** With Florida HB 3 now enforceable and the NGL order setting an 18+ precedent, a consumer anonymous product should plan to be 18+ with a real age gate. If minors are allowed, it must be COPPA-compliant under the 2025 rule and handle state-by-state parental consent.
- **B2B.** Workplace and compliance anonymous reporting is the one segment where law creates demand rather than just risk.

### Gaps

- Specific provisions of the 2025 COPPA amendments (separate consent for third-party disclosure, data-retention policy, security program, broader "personal information") were not fetched in detail.

  - Dodd-Frank SEC whistleblower anonymity (via counsel)

## Q5. Patterns: what survived vs. faded; retention data

### Takeaway
The anonymous products that lasted are tied to a bounded, identity-verified community or a specific job: campus-verified feeds (Sidechat/Yik Yak 2.0, Fizz), professional or compliance use (SOX/Guidelines-driven hotlines), and Whisper (long-lived but shrunk, with a damaging 2020 leak). Open, teen-targeted "anonymous compliments / ask me anything" apps peaked within months and were shut down, acquired-and-killed, removed, or sued.

### Cited Findings
- Timelines from peak to shutdown or decline:
  - Gas: launched August 2022, downloads peaked November 2022, shut down November 2023 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/19/discord-kills-gas-anonymous-compliments-app-bought-nine-months-ago)
  - tbh: acquired October 2017, killed July 2018 for low usage — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/07/02/facebook-is-shutting-down-hello-moves-and-the-anonymous-teen-app-tbh-due-to-low-usage)
  - Secret: shut down after 16 months — [CNN Money](https://money.cnn.com/2015/04/29/technology/secret-closes-investors/)
  - Yik Yak: about 3 years from peak to closure — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/08/16/yik-yak-is-back/)
- A Mobile Dev Memo analyst argued that anonymous apps struggle to drive engagement beyond a niche audience. — [Mobile Dev Memo](https://mobiledevmemo.com/the-importance-of-facebook-shuttering-tbh-9-months-after-acquiring-it/)
- The post-2021 campus apps that survive gate on school email. That cost them Yik Yak's open user base but fits bounded communities. — [TechCrunch, 2023-04-03](https://techcrunch.com/2023/04/03/yik-yak-users-are-protesting-the-apps-makeover-after-acquisition-by-sidechat/amp/)
- NGL survived its FTC order and still shows meaningful downloads and revenue on Sensor Tower, now as an 18+ app. — [Sensor Tower](https://app.sensortower.com/overview/1596550932?country=US); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/ftc-bans-anonymous-messaging-app-ngl-for-minors-rcna160897)

### Inferences
- Three things look like they predict durability:
  1. A verified, bounded audience (a school, a company, a team).
  2. A recurring use case beyond curiosity (campus news and marketplace, workplace feedback, compliance reporting).
  3. Monetization that doesn't depend on "who sent this?" reveals.
- Products built on a one-time curiosity loop plus teen distribution show about 3–12-month half-lives.
- For saysecretly, the safest framing is either 18+ consumer use with honest safety claims and no fake engagement, or a B2B/community tool where anonymity serves a defined reporting or feedback purpose.

### Gaps
- I found no published cohort retention data (D1/D30) specific to anonymous social apps. Sensor Tower and Appfigures retention figures for NGL, Gas or Sendit were not located.
- No academic retention studies were found in this session.
- Academic cyberbullying literature on anonymous apps was not reviewed.
