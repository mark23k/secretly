# B2B Anonymous Feedback and Reporting Tools: US Market for "Say Secretly" (saysecretly.com)

Research date: 2026-10-27. Prices are in USD unless noted. Many price figures come from vendor-authored comparison blogs or third-party estimates rather than official price lists, and each one is labeled. Market-size figures come from paywalled analyst reports, quoted through reseller summaries; the reports define their markets differently, so their figures vary widely.

## 1. Market sizes and growth rates

### Takeaway
All three markets are real but their size estimates disagree. Employee engagement software is about $0.8–1.1B globally (2024–2025), growing at 6–16% a year. Whistleblowing software is somewhere between $0.28B and $2.7B (2025), growing at 5–12% a year. Voice-of-customer (VoC) software is the largest at about $2.5–8B (2024–2025), growing at about 15–17% a year. None of the sources gives a US-only figure.

### Cited Findings
**Employee engagement software**
- Grand View Research: global market about $928.3M in 2023, with a 16.4% CAGR forecast for 2024–2030 (2023 base, so older data) — [GII reseller summary of GVR report](https://giikorea.co.kr/report/grvi1575265-employee-engagement-software-market-size-share.html)
- IMARC: $1,068.0M in 2024, reaching $2,964.8M by 2033 (11.9% CAGR for 2025–2033) — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/employee-engagement-software-market-statistics)
- Value Market Research: $0.79B in 2024, reaching $2.09B by 2033 (11.43% CAGR) — [GII / VMR](https://giikorea.co.kr/report/vmr1782240-global-employee-engagement-software-market.html)
- Market Glass: $828.8M in 2025, reaching $1.2B by 2032 (5.9% CAGR) — [Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/5980692/employee-engagement-software-global-strategic)

**Whistleblowing software** (estimates conflict by about 10x)
- Coherent Market Insights: about $270M in 2026, reaching $380M by 2033 (5.1% CAGR) — [GII / Coherent](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/coh2084756-whistleblowing-software-market-by-deployment-type.html)
- Verified Market Reports: $0.279B in 2025, 6.5% CAGR for 2026–2033 — [Verified Market Reports](https://www.verifiedmarketreports.com/product/whistleblowing-software-market/)
- Market Research Intellect: $392M in 2025, reaching $1.22B by 2035 (12% CAGR) — [MRI](https://www.marketresearchintellect.com/product/whistleblowing-software-professional-market/)
- WiseGuy Reports: $2.73B in 2025, reaching $5.5B by 2035 (7.2% CAGR). This is probably a broader definition that includes compliance and case-management suites — [WiseGuy](https://www.wiseguyreports.com/reports/whistleblowing-software-market)
- A Veremark blog figure ($123B in 2024) is far out of line with every other source and should be discarded — [Veremark](https://veremark.com/blog/the-4-6b-whistleblowing-wake-up-call)

**Voice of customer (VoC) software**
- Mordor Intelligence: $8.03B in 2025 and $9.18B in 2026, reaching $19.83B by 2031 (16.65% CAGR); software was about 72% of the market — [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/voice-of-customer-software-market)
- King's Research: the broader VoC market was $23.86B in 2025, reaching $62.59B by 2032; its software segment was $13.35B in 2024 — [King's Research](https://www.kingsresearch.com/report/voice-of-customer-voc-market-2683)
- A February 2025 press release gives $2.46B in 2024, reaching $7.7B by 2032 (15.3% CAGR) — cited via search summary only; the original release was not retrieved

**Usage benchmarks for whistleblowing hotlines (NAVEX 2025, based on 2024 data)**
- US-based organizations had a median of 1.78 reports per 100 employees per year (global median 1.57; Europe 0.67) — [NAVEX 2025 US key findings](https://cdn.navex.com/image/upload/v1748609318/resources-2025/benchmarking-reports/navex-2025-regional-bmk-whistleblowing-key-findings_US_EN.pdf)
- Median anonymous reporting rate: 54% globally and about 51–52% in the US/North America — [NAVEX benchmark](https://www.navex.com/en-us/northstar/whistleblowing-statistics-benchmark-report/)
- Web intake (33.4%) overtook phone hotline intake (29.4%) for the first time. Median time to close a case rose from 21 to 28 days — [NAVEX 2025 report PDF](https://cdn.navex.com/image/upload/v1742410133/resources-2025/benchmarking-reports/2025-navex-whistleblowing-incident-mgt-benchmark-report.pdf)

### Inferences
- About half of hotline reports are anonymous, and web intake has now passed phone. That favors a web-first, anonymous-first product over a staffed call center.
- At about 1.8 reports per 100 employees a year, a 50-person company might get roughly one report a year. A pure whistleblowing product for small businesses therefore sells compliance and insurance rather than daily use, which argues for bundling it with an everyday suggestion box or pulse survey to keep engagement and perceived value up.
- The whistleblowing market is small, perhaps a few hundred million dollars for pure-play software, but a bootstrapped product needs only a tiny share of it.

### Gaps
- I found no US-only market size for any of the three categories. All figures are global.
- I found no market size for anonymous suggestion-box tools, school tip lines, or upward/360 feedback as standalone categories.
- I could not see the full analyst reports or their methodology; all figures come from summaries.

## 2. Competitors and pricing

### Takeaway
Prices split into three tiers. Enterprise suites (NAVEX, OneTrust/Convercent, Vault, Culture Amp, Lattice, Peakon) sell by quote with minimums of about $4–10K a year. Mid-market per-employee tools (Officevibe, 15Five, FaceUp, Whistleblower Software) charge about $1–5 per employee per month. Flat-fee small-business tools (Safe Hotline, FraudHL, Lighthouse, Incogneato, Polly) charge about $250–1,200 a year. Self-serve, transparently priced options with real two-way anonymous chat are few.

### Inferences
- What a small business can afford: for a 10–200 person company, the observed self-serve price points are $500–600 a year flat for whistleblowing (Safe Hotline, FraudHL), $49–99/month flat for anonymous suggestion boxes with chat (Incogneato), or about $1–5 per user per month for pulse surveys. A flat price ladder of roughly $29 / $79 / $199 a month would sit inside these anchors. That ladder is my extrapolation, not a sourced figure.
- Many of the leading anonymity-focused tools (AllVoices, Suggestion Ox, FaceUp, Vault, NAVEX) hide prices behind a demo. Transparent, self-serve pricing with instant setup is a real point of difference for small businesses.
- Two-way anonymous chat is now table stakes: FaceUp includes it on every plan, Incogneato on its lowest paid tier, and AllVoices markets "encrypted messaging."

### Gaps
- Pricing for WhistleB (now part of NAVEX), Speeqr, Spot (talktospot.com), and the current Lattice and Culture Amp list prices could not be confirmed from primary sources. "EthicalPoint" appears to be a confusion with NAVEX EthicsPoint.
- Most per-employee estimates come from vendor-authored comparison blogs (VoxWel, Matter, CultureMonkey, FeedbackPulse) that have a commercial bias.
- I found no published pricing for guest-feedback QR tools.

## 3. Underserved segments

### Takeaway
The clearest gap is the 10–250 employee US business that has a legal or practical reason to offer an anonymous channel (a CA SB 553 workplace violence plan, federal subcontracts, healthcare compliance, a franchise, or nonprofit governance) but cannot justify enterprise quotes or demo-gated sales. Within that group, high-turnover frontline workforces such as restaurants, retail, and clinics are poorly served by desk-centric, Slack-based, per-seat tools.

### Cited Findings
- Flat-fee vendors explicitly target small businesses and nonprofits (Safe Hotline: under 250 employees at $600 or $500 a year; FraudHL: "Whistleblower Hotline for Small Business & Nonprofits"), which shows demand exists below the enterprise tier — [Safe Hotline](https://safehotline.com/); [FraudHL](https://www.fraudhl.com/)
- AllVoices built a separate "Small Business Whistleblower Hotline" purchase page and markets SB 553 compliance, which signals that vendors see small-business demand driven by SB 553 — [AllVoices](https://www.allvoices.co/basic-purchase)
- CA SB 553 applies to private employers with 10 or more employees at publicly accessible locations, which covers most restaurants, retail stores, and clinics in California — [Clym](https://regulations.clym.io/california-senate-bill-no-553)
- NAVEX-class tools carry $5–10K+ annual fees and setup costs of $1K–25K+ on multi-year contracts (competitor estimate) — [VoxWel](https://voxwel.com/blogs/whistleblowing-software-pricing-comparison)
- For frontline staff, the cheapest current answer is a free web form opened from a QR code in the break room (no app or login), which shows the low-friction pattern buyers want — [freesuggestionbox.com](https://freesuggestionbox.com/employee-suggestion-box). In a small crew, writing style or timing can still reveal who submitted, a weakness these tools do not address — [search summary of suggestion-box guides](https://suggestionbox.tips/guide/online-suggestion-box)
- Schools: the dominant K-12 tip-line product is often free to districts through state grants and consortia, so direct-to-school pricing power for student safety tip lines is weak — [Ohio School Plan](https://ohioschoolplan.org/focusing-on-safety/stopit-solutions/); [StopIt SERRC](https://www.stopitsolutions.com/serrc). Incogneato and Suggestion Ox already sell to schools with education discounts — [Incogneato](https://www.incognea.to/); [Suggestion Ox](https://suggestionox.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- Best-fit beachhead: California small and mid-size employers with frontline staff (restaurants, retail, clinics, franchises), combining a QR or SMS anonymous channel, two-way chat, a case log, and SB 553 and EEOC compliance positioning at a flat $29–99/month. Franchisors could buy one plan per location.
- Nonprofits and churches face governance pressure (IRS Form 990 asks whether a whistleblower policy exists) and are served by $500/year flat hotlines. This could be a secondary segment. The Form 990 point comes from my background knowledge and was not verified in this research.
- Schools as a student safety market look crowded and subsidized. Staff or teacher feedback, and parent-to-school anonymous feedback, may be less contested, but I found no evidence either way.
- Guest feedback ("tell us privately before you post") is crowded and carries policy risk (see the gaps below). A sharper angle is anonymous staff feedback for the same hospitality buyers.

### Gaps
- I found no survey data quantifying how many US small businesses have, or want, an anonymous channel.
- I found no Reddit (r/smallbusiness or r/humanresources) threads in the results; buyer sentiment there is unverified.
- I found no data on churches or franchise owners specifically.
- Review gating (sending only happy customers to Google) may conflict with Google's review policies and the FTC's 2024 rule on consumer reviews. I did not verify this in this research, and it should be checked before building a guest-feedback product.

## 4. US legal drivers

### Takeaway
The only explicit US mandate for an anonymous channel is SOX §301, which applies to public companies' audit committees. FAR 52.203-13 (federal contractors) requires an internal reporting mechanism that is anonymous or confidential. DOJ's corporate compliance guidance and HHS-OIG's healthcare guidance strongly expect anonymity without strictly mandating it. CA SB 553 (since July 2024) requires confidential reporting procedures for most CA employers with 10 or more employees; sources dispute whether anonymity is required. The EU Directive requires confidential channels for each EU entity with 50 or more workers, and whether anonymous reports must be accepted is left to each member state.

### Cited Findings
- **SOX §301 (§10A(m)(4))**: audit committees of listed companies must establish procedures for complaints about accounting, internal controls, and auditing, including "the confidential, anonymous submission by employees" of concerns. Most issuers had to comply by 2004 and small-business issuers by 2005 — [Northfield Bank whistleblower policy (2025) citing §301(m)(4)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/311495203/files/doc_governance/2025/Oct/24/NFBK-Whistleblower-Policy-10222025-2e5990.pdf); [Ethico](https://www.ethico.com/insights/sarbanes-oxley-whistleblower-program-requirements-what-sox-section-301-actually-demands-from-your-hotline-2); [Perkins Coie](https://www.perkinscoie.com/en/news-insights/whistleblower-provisions-of-the-sarbanes-oxley-act-some.html)
- **DOJ Evaluation of Corporate Compliance Programs (ECCP)**: asks prosecutors "Does the company have an anonymous reporting mechanism and, if not, why not?" The 2024 update emphasizes whistleblower anti-retaliation and well-publicized channels — [NAVEX blog](https://www.navex.com/blog/article/how-to-get-the-most-out-of-your-confidential-hotline/); [Compliance Podcast Network on the 2024 ECCP](https://compliancepodcastnetwork.net/argentieri-speech-and-2024-eccp-whistleblowers-and-anti-retaliation/)
- **FAR 52.203-13 (federal contractors)**: requires an internal control system that includes a reporting mechanism, such as a hotline, that allows employees to report anonymously or confidentially (vendor summary; check 48 CFR 52.203-13) — [FaceUp](https://www.faceup.com/en/laws-and-regulations/us-contractor-code-of-business-ethics-and-conduct)
- **HHS-OIG compliance guidance (healthcare)**: calls for a process such as a hotline to receive complaints, with procedures to protect complainants' anonymity (2020 secondary source) — [Compliance.com (2020)](https://www.compliance.com/?p=14822)
- **CA SB 553 / Labor Code §6401.9**: effective July 1, 2024. Requires a written workplace violence prevention plan, an incident log, and training. One summary lists anonymous or confidential reporting as a responsibility; another says only confidential reporting is mandatory and anonymous reporting is suggested. Cal/OSHA's standard is due to be adopted by December 31, 2026 — [Clym](https://regulations.clym.io/california-senate-bill-no-553); contradicted by [Spot (talktospot.com)](https://talktospot.com/sb553); [WorldatWork](https://worldatwork.org/publications/workspan-daily/california-s-workplace-violence-law-could-be-a-trend-setter); [Ethena](https://www.goethena.com/post/does-an-anonymous-form-meet-california-sb-553-requirements/)
- **EU Whistleblowing Directive 2019/1937**: each legal entity with 50 or more workers needs its own internal channel that keeps the reporter's identity confidential. Member states decide whether anonymous reports must be accepted; Spain requires it, and Belgium does for companies with 250 or more workers. US groups are affected through their EU subsidiaries — [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/1937/oj/eng); [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/trending-topics/the-eu-whistleblowing-directive); [search summary of DLA Piper and Safework guides](https://safework.place/guides/eu-whistleblowing-directive/)
- Research caveat: one study found that introducing an anonymous channel may decrease reporting intentions across all channels — [Monash University](https://research.monash.edu/en/publications/an-examination-of-the-effects-of-procedural-safeguards-on-intenti/)

### Inferences
- For small private US businesses, the "legal need" is mostly indirect: defensibility in EEOC, harassment, and retaliation claims; SB 553 in California; FAR flow-down for government subcontractors; and healthcare compliance programs. The sales message is "documented, confidential channel plus case log" more than "legally mandated anonymity."
- Dodd-Frank creates SEC whistleblower awards that give companies an incentive to capture concerns internally first. It does not itself mandate an internal anonymous channel. This point is from background knowledge and was not verified by a source in this research.

### Gaps
- I did not verify primary text for Dodd-Frank §922 or the SEC whistleblower rules, OSHA §11(c), or other state laws (for example NY Labor Law §740 as amended in 2022, or state K-12 tip-line statutes such as Colorado's Safe2Tell and Michigan's OK2SAY). Any statement about those should be checked.
- No source confirmed whether FAR requires a hotline poster.

## 5. Buyer complaints and needs (reviews)

### Takeaway
The recurring buyer and user concern is trust: whether "anonymous" really means anonymous or just "internally confidential." Next come the inability to follow up on anonymous tips (which two-way anonymous chat solves), demo-gated or opaque pricing, and Slack/Teams add-on fees.

### Cited Findings
- Employees question "are anonymous surveys really anonymous?", which reduces how openly they respond — [CultureMonkey](https://www.culturemonkey.io/guides/anonymous-employee-feedback/open-anonymous-feedback/pros-and-cons-of-anonymous-employee-feedback/)
- HR teams report having to train staff hard to trust a feedback module, and staff stay wary of whether feedback is "truly anonymous" or only "internally confidential" — [ProProfs roundup](https://www.proprofssurvey.com/blog/best-anonymous-feedback-tools/)
- Confidentiality concerns recur in user feedback on Microsoft Viva Glint — [Apollo Technical](https://www.apollotechnical.com/best-anonymous-employee-feedback-tools-for-enterprise-teams/)
- Anonymous channels make follow-up hard, because managers cannot ask clarifying questions and reports lack detail — [CultureMonkey](https://www.culturemonkey.io/guides/anonymous-employee-feedback/open-anonymous-feedback/pros-and-cons-of-anonymous-employee-feedback/)
- Vendors answer this with two-way anonymous chat: FaceUp's 16-digit case key with encrypted messaging, and Incogneato's email relay or conversation code — [FaceUp](https://www.faceup.com/en/pricing); [Incogneato](https://www.incognea.to/)
- Pricing friction: Qualtrics and TINYpulse sell Slack/Teams delivery as add-ons outside the standard license, and many tools are quote-gated — [Matter blog](https://matterapp.com/blog/tinypulse-alternatives)
- Officevibe reportedly requires a sales call with no self-serve plan (one source) — [FeedbackPulse](https://feedbackpulse.com/resources/best-employee-survey-software)

### Inferences
- Product requirements that follow: provable anonymity (no IP logging, a published technical explanation, metadata stripping, delayed or batched delivery to defeat timing inference, and minimum group sizes before results show), two-way anonymous chat by default, setup in 5 minutes with a QR code and link, and a public flat price.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve actual G2 or Capterra review text; the review sites did not appear in search results. The findings above come from secondary roundups, many vendor-written.

## 6. Evidence of solo or small founders succeeding

### Takeaway
I found no verified revenue figure (MRR or ARR) for a solo or indie anonymous-feedback SaaS. The best evidence is that several small, apparently bootstrapped vendors survive on flat low prices (Incogneato at $49–599/month under Corpell LLC; Safe Hotline at $600/year; FraudHL at $500/year; freesuggestionbox.com and suggestionbox.tips with freemium models), which suggests the niche supports small operators.

### Cited Findings
- Incogneato is run by Corpell LLC, publishes self-serve pricing, and shows logos of large organizations — [Incogneato](https://www.incognea.to/)
- Safe Hotline and FraudHL operate on flat $500–600/year small-business pricing — [Safe Hotline](https://safehotline.com/); [FraudHL](https://www.fraudhl.com/)
- Base rates: about 50% of active indie hackers earn $0–1K MRR and about 20% earn $1K–10K (a rough 2026 blog estimate) — [BetterLaunch](https://www.betterlaunch.co/blog/indie-hacker). A self-reported analysis of 500 SaaS Product Hunt launches from early 2024 found 97.4% under $1K MRR months later — [FunnelKeeper](https://funnelkeeper.com/blog/product-hunt/)

### Inferences
- Revenue math: about 200 small-business customers at $50/month is about $10K MRR. That is plausible given the competitor price anchors, but unproven.

### Gaps
- No Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, or Reddit case study with disclosed revenue for an anonymous feedback tool was found. Targeted searches did not surface any (for example "Incogneato revenue", "Suggestion Ox founder", "Sendbird anonymous", or Product Hunt "anonymous feedback" launches).
- I found no revenue data for Incogneato, Suggestion Ox, Safe Hotline, or FraudHL.
