# Secretly ad: ElevenLabs voice-over

About 30 seconds. Paste ONE of the two versions below into ElevenLabs **Text to Speech**.

## Version A: Eleven Multilingual v2 (most reliable)

Copy everything inside the box:

```
Everyone has a thought they've never said out loud. <break time="0.6s" />

On Secretly, you swipe through the ones other people finally shared... <break time="0.4s" />
and find out you were never the only one. <break time="0.8s" />

No names. No profiles. <break time="0.3s" /> Just honest people. <break time="0.8s" />

Got a question you could never ask anyone you know? <break time="0.4s" />
Ask it here, and get real answers from people who've been there. <break time="0.8s" />

And every day, you can give a secret... <break time="0.5s" />
and get a stranger's secret back. <break time="0.6s" />

Send them one kind message. <break time="0.3s" /> That's it. <break time="0.8s" />

Three a day. <break time="0.4s" /> Then you'll want to come back tomorrow. <break time="1.0s" />

Secretly. <break time="0.4s" /> Say the thing you'd never say out loud.
```

## Version B: Eleven v3 (more emotional, supports performance tags)

v3 understands tags in square brackets and doesn't use break tags. Copy everything inside the box:

```
[softly] Everyone has a thought they've never said out loud...

On Secretly, you swipe through the ones other people finally shared... [gentle pause] and find out you were never the only one.

No names. No profiles. Just honest people.

[curious] Got a question you could never ask anyone you know? Ask it here, and get real answers from people who've been there.

[whispers] And every day, you can give a secret... [softly] and get a stranger's secret back.

Send them one kind message. That's it.

[warmly] Three a day. Then you'll want to come back tomorrow.

[softly] Secretly. Say the thing you'd never say out loud.
```

## Voice

Pick a **soft, warm, intimate** voice. It should sound like a friend telling you something quietly at night, not an announcer. In the Voice Library, search for **"calm"**, **"soft"**, **"intimate"** or **"storytelling"**, and preview a few with the first line.

- A young-adult voice (20s to 30s) fits the audience best.
- Avoid deep "movie trailer" or bright "radio ad" voices.

## Settings (Multilingual v2)

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | 0.90 to 0.95 | Slightly slow feels intimate |
| Stability | 45 to 55% | Natural rise and fall without wobbling |
| Similarity | 75% | Stays true to the voice |
| Style exaggeration | 15 to 25% | A little emotion; more starts sounding acted |
| Speaker boost | On | Clearer on phone speakers |

For v3, start with the **Natural** stability preset.

## Tips

- Generate 2 or 3 takes and keep the best. Every take is slightly different.
- Download as MP3, drop it into CapCut, and place each frame where its line starts:

| Frame | Starts on the line |
|---|---|
| 01-home | "Everyone has a thought…" |
| 02-swipe | "On Secretly, you swipe…" |
| 03-result | "…and find out you were never the only one." |
| 04-feed | "No names. No profiles." |
| 05-ask | "Got a question…" |
| 06-answers | "Ask it here…" |
| 07-write-secret | "And every day, you can give a secret…" |
| 08-sealed | "…and get a stranger's secret back." |
| 09-opened | "Send them one kind message." |
| 10-come-back-tomorrow | "Three a day." |
| 11-end-card | "Secretly. Say the thing…" |

- Keep the music low (about 15 to 20% volume) so the voice stays on top.
- If one line sounds off, regenerate just that line and swap it in, rather than redoing the whole take.
